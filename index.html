The Set

Tonight's Program

Nine selections from the contemporary big band repertoire, in the order they will be performed.

Side A Five Tracks · ~26 min
  1. 01
    Bloo Phunk Strut
    Michele Fernandez
    Featuring · Tenor Saxophone, Lead Trumpet
    4:45

    Grammy-nominated composer and educator Michele Fernandez has become one of the defining voices in contemporary big band writing. 'Bloo Phunk Strut' lives in the territory where blues meets funk, opening the night with the kind of pocket and pulse that wakes a room up.

  2. 02
    Movin' Uptown
    Benny Carter
    Featuring · Alto Saxophone, Trumpet
    5:30

    Benny Carter (1907 to 2003), alto saxophonist, trumpeter, arranger, and bandleader, wrote and arranged across nearly eight decades and is widely regarded as the architect of the modern saxophone section. 'Movin' Uptown' is a clinic in ensemble swing and sectional precision.

  3. 03
    Fresh Start
    Marshall Gilkes
    Featuring · Trombone, Piano
    6:15

    Contemporary trombonist and composer Marshall Gilkes, whose work with Germany's WDR Big Band has reshaped the modern jazz orchestra, brings the harmonic depth and rhythmic intricacy of his small-group writing to the big band format in 'Fresh Start.'

  4. 04
    A Child Is Born
    Thad Jones · arr. Bob Brookmeyer
    Featuring · Flugelhorn, Piano
    5:30

    Thad Jones' 1969 ballad has become one of the most-recorded standards in the modern big band canon. The chord movement and unmistakable melody make this a fixture of the literature, and a moment of repose mid-program.

    Selection to be confirmed by the directors.

  5. 05
    Cute
    Neal Hefti
    Featuring · Drum Set
    4:15

    Composed by Neal Hefti in 1958 and made famous by Count Basie, 'Cute' is one of the most beloved drum features in the big band literature. The chart's economy and the drummer's brushwork carry the whole tune.

    Selection to be confirmed by the directors.

Side B Four Tracks · ~24 min
  1. 06
    Roy Allan
    Roy Hargrove
    Featuring · Trumpet, Tenor Saxophone
    6:00

    Roy Hargrove (1969 to 2018) was one of the defining trumpet voices of his generation, equally compelling in straight-ahead, Afro-Cuban, and contemporary settings. 'Roy Allan' showcases the lyricism and rhythmic poise that made his playing unmistakable.

  2. 07
    Niner Two
    Bob Mintzer
    Featuring · Tenor Saxophone, Bass
    5:45

    Bob Mintzer is a Grammy-winning saxophonist, arranger, and bandleader whose charts have been performed by every major jazz orchestra of the past four decades. 'Niner Two' is Mintzer in his signature mode: harmonically rich, rhythmically tight, demanding ensemble precision at every turn.

    Selection to be confirmed by the directors.

  3. 08
    Sing Sang Sung
    Gordon Goodwin
    Featuring · Full Ensemble
    5:15

    Gordon Goodwin's Big Phat Band has redefined what a modern jazz orchestra can sound like. 'Sing Sang Sung' fuses Basie-style swing with contemporary rhythmic and harmonic vocabulary, a fitting prelude to the night's Latin closer.

    Selection to be confirmed by the directors.

  4. 09
    Veneros
    Joe Gallardo · arr. Michael Philip Mossman
    Featuring · Trumpet, Percussion
    6:30

    Composed by Texas-based Latin jazz veteran Joe Gallardo and arranged by Michael Philip Mossman, the internationally recognized trumpeter and one of the leading voices in Latin big band writing, 'Veneros' brings Afro-Cuban energy and layered percussion to the closing of the night.

The Program

About City High Jazz

The City High Jazz Ensemble is the flagship of a band program with deep roots in Iowa music education. Founded as part of one of the first comprehensive instrumental programs in the state, the ensemble has trained generations of Iowa City musicians and sent students on to study at conservatories and university music schools across the country.

Under the direction of Mr. Aaron Ottmar, the City High Jazz Ensemble has built a reputation as one of the strongest high school jazz programs in Iowa. The ensemble has been crowned Iowa Jazz Champions in two of the past three years — capturing the Class 4A title at the Iowa Jazz Championships in both 2024 and 2026, and earning the right to represent the state's largest classification on the IBA stage tonight.

City High Jazz is grateful to the band parents, private instructors, school administrators, and members of the Iowa City community whose support over decades has made this evening possible.

The Program

Program at a Glance

Five things to know about jazz at City High.

Depth

Four Jazz Ensembles

Four extracurricular jazz ensembles run inside the City High music program, giving students room to grow across all four years.

Access

Open Placement

Every student who completes a September audition earns a spot in a jazz band — no one is turned away from the program.

Tradition

Annual Chicago Trip

Each year, the jazz program travels to Chicago to study, listen, and play in one of the great American jazz cities.

Showcase

A Professional Guest, Every Year

An annual collaboration with a working jazz musician anchors the City High Jazz Showcase — bringing the profession into the band room.

Recognition

Recent Guest Stages

City High Jazz has recently been invited to perform alongside the Iowa Jazz Composers Orchestra, UNI Jazz Band One, and the University of Iowa Johnson County Landmark Ensembles — peers from across Iowa's professional and collegiate jazz scene.
